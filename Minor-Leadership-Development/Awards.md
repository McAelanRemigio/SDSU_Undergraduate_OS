## 2026 Homecoming Royal — Leadership & Service

**San Diego State University** · October 2026

Selected as one of two 2026 SDSU Homecoming Royals from a court of 10 student leaders. This distinction recognizes leadership, academic achievement, and community impact. The selection process included a written service proposal and interview presentation. The two selected Royals will lead a campus-wide service project in Spring 2027.
